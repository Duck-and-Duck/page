Beyond basic auto-attendants.

Most AI voice agents hallucinate, interrupt, and fail to book. Bative agents are engineered with strict tool-calling guardrails and vertical-specific knowledge bases.

01

Real-time Calendar Sync

Agents execute atomic API calls to your calendar or CRM. No hallucinations, no double-bookings. The agent always runs a fresh availability check immediately before committing a slot.

02

Interruption Handling

Native voice-activity detection ensures the agent stops speaking the millisecond the caller interrupts.

03

Smart Escalation

Complex queries or emergencies are instantly routed to your mobile device via call forwarding or warm transfer.

04

Post-Call Summaries

Instant SMS/email summaries triggered immediately after the call disconnects. Every conversation logged.

System Architecture

Engineered for reliability, low latency, and strict tool-calling guardrails.

Telephony Ingest

Twilio trunking routes inbound calls to our voice platform. Keep your existing business number — just forward calls to the agent.

Tool Execution
